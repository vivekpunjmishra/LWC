Safety First!

Thank you for your participation in the first waves of our food safety audits.

As a reminder, each store will be required to have at least one ServSafe (or equivalent) certified employee. To prepare, we recommend having employees complete training and earn their certification in 2019. You can use the ServSafe discounted pricing negotiated by Domino’s.

You can read more about this new standard in section 1.16 of the Food Safety Reference Guide.

To check whether another vendor’s certification would qualify for this requirement, please reach out to your QA market consultant.
